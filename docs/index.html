LLM-powered smart home prototype

LumeEdge makes a small room think for itself.

A connected home agent that reads temperature, humidity, brightness, time, weather, and personal preferences, then uses an LLM to decide how much light and ventilation the space needs.

A complete edge-to-cloud home agent.

LumeEdge combines embedded sensing, Node-RED orchestration, an LLM-powered reasoning service, and physical actuators into one compact smart-home demonstration.

S

Sensor awareness

Environmental data flows from the embedded board into the control service, giving the agent a live view of comfort and lighting conditions.

L

LLM reasoning

The model converts user preference, sensor readings, time, and weather context into explainable control decisions.

P

Polished UI

The screen presents everything clearly and accessibly, including battery level, Wi-Fi status, sensor readings, and the AI’s reasoning.

U

User control

The dashboard supports preference entry, manual override, LLM decision display, and OTA update access from a mobile-friendly interface.

Designed around explainable autonomy.

Instead of only toggling devices, LumeEdge tells the user why it adjusted the environment. The same loop can support comfort, energy savings, and remote maintenance.

Sense

The embedded system reports indoor temperature, humidity, and brightness.

Personalize

The dashboard collects comfort targets, day and night light preferences, and user-defined instructions.

Reason

The LLM evaluates conditions with time and weather tools, then returns brightness, ventilation, and a concise reason.

Control

The service exposes clean decision endpoints for Node-RED and firmware to consume.

Actuate

The MCU receives control signals from Node-RED and executes them through its internal logic.

Update

OTA support keeps the firmware iteration loop open after the prototype is assembled.

Built as a physical smart-room model.

The prototype packages sensors, display, battery, wiring, lighting, and ventilation into a tangible room-scale demonstration, making the AI output visible and testable.

Future Plan:

Looking ahead, we plan to further miniaturize the PCB to fit within the footprint of a standard wall outlet. We will also design a custom 3D-printed enclosure that blends naturally with the wall environment, making the device easy to install, visually unobtrusive, and suitable for everyday use in real-world settings.
